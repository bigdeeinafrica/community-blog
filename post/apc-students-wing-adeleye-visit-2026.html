APC Remo North Campaign Council Inaugurated, Students Wing Pays Solidarity Visit to Adeleye

Newly recognized Students Wing, led by Comrade Megbowon Olusola Ezekiel, receives charge on party's 5-Direct agenda

Community Desk · 22 September 2026 · APC Party Secretariat, Odemo Road, Alagomeji, Isara-Remo, Ogun State

Members of the Students Wing Committee with Hon. Adebiyi Adeleye at the party secretariat.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) Remo North Local Government Campaign Council was formally inaugurated on Tuesday at the APC Party Secretariat, Odemo Road, Alagomeji, Isara-Remo.

At the inauguration, the Secretary of the Ogun State APC Campaign Council, Hon. Babatunde Adejare, charged party leaders, members, and committees to work toward securing victory for all APC candidates, restating the party's "5-Direct" mandate across every level of the ticket.

A key part of the inauguration was the formal recognition of the Campaign Council's Students Wing Committee, tasked with engaging, mobilizing, and sensitizing students across Remo North Local Government ahead of the election.

Following the inauguration, the Students Wing leadership paid a solidarity and courtesy visit to the APC House of Assembly candidate for Remo North State Constituency, Sir (Hon.) Adebiyi Adeleye, KJW.

Receiving the delegation, Adeleye thanked the students for turning out in strength and for their support, and urged the Students Wing to keep developing into responsible future leaders while stepping up mobilization for APC candidates among students.

He listed the candidates the group was charged to campaign for: President Bola Ahmed Tinubu; Senator Adeola Solomon Olamilekan Yayi for Governor of Ogun State; Prince Dr. Dapo Abiodun for Senator, Ogun East; Hon. Funmilayo Efuape for the Remo Federal Constituency seat in the House of Representatives; and Adeleye himself for the Remo North State Constituency seat in the House of Assembly.

Adeleye described the coming election in regional terms and argued for Tinubu's re-election in 2027 to build on reforms begun in 2023, while framing Yayi's prospective governorship as a continuation of Dapo Abiodun's development record and Efuape's candidacy as important for Remo's continued advancement — framing consistent with the campaign's own messaging.

The visit was attended by leadership from the National Association of Remo Students (NARS), the YAYI Students Vanguard (Remo North Chapter), the Students Wing Committee of the Remo North LG Campaign Council, NAOSS OOU Chapter, and a former GAPOSA SUG President. The delegation was led by Students Wing Committee Chairman Comrade Megbowon Olusola Ezekiel, alongside NARS National President Comrade Sarfardeen Sopitan and YAYI Students Vanguard (Remo North) Coordinator Comrade Alao Toheeb Oluwafunmito.

The press statement announcing the visit was signed by Comrade Megbowon Olusola Ezekiel, Acting Chairman of the Students Wing Committee, and Prince Sarafadeen Sopitan, NARS President and Acting Secretary of the Campaign Council.
