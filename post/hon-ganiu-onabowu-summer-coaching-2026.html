250 Isara pupils graduate from free holiday coaching programme

Season 2 of the Ward 1 initiative wraps up with scholarships, textbooks, and cash prizes

Community Desk · 4 September 2026 · St. Peter's School, Isara, Remo North LGA, Ogun State

Powered by Hon. Ganiu Onabowu T., Majority Leader, Remo North Legislative Council

Pupils show off their new school bags and textbooks at the close of the programme.

Two hundred and fifty primary school pupils in Ward 1, Isara, have completed the second season of a free holiday coaching and scholarship scheme, closing out four weeks of extra academic support over the long vacation.

The programme ran from August 10 to September 4 at St. Peter's School, Isara, on weekday mornings from 9am to noon, open to pupils in Primary 3 through Primary 6. Sessions covered Mathematics, English Language, and Basic/Integrated Science, led by experienced teachers using structured lesson notes and continuous assessment.

Giving an overview of the programme at the closing ceremony, monitoring officer Mr Dada David said the scheme was designed to give pupils free access to quality academic support over the holidays, strengthen their grasp of core subjects, and close learning gaps that come from limited access to extra lessons outside school.

He reported attendance above 90 percent for the full four weeks, along with measurable improvement between pre- and post-programme assessments across all three subjects. Beyond the numbers, he pointed to a visible boost in pupils' confidence and enthusiasm for learning, and said the full holiday syllabus was completed on schedule.

Sixteen pupils who placed first through fifth in their grade, from Primary 3 to Primary 6, received Mathematics and English textbooks. Twenty pupils recognized for good conduct went home with school bags, and four pupils named as overall top performers each received a cash prize of ₦10,000.

Two pupils were named scholarship recipients: Adetola Esther Oluwatosin, awarded a one-year scholarship, and Abiodun Maria Ebunoluwa, awarded a two-year scholarship, both of St. Peter Nur/Pry School.

The closing ceremony was attended by a number of local dignitaries, including Prince Adeyinka Adeleye, Alhaji Lukman representing the Chairman of Remo North LGA, Hon. Oluwaseyi Esho (Deputy Leader, Remo North Legislative Council), Hon. Ahmed Akinyemi, High Chief Afolabi Onabowu, and Hon. Dada Ayodeji, among others.

Closing out his remarks, Mr Dada David thanked Hon. Ganiu Onabowu T., Majority Leader of the Remo North Legislative Council and councillor for Ward 1, for the initiative, describing it as a demonstration of what free, quality education can do to change young lives.
